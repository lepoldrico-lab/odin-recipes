Burger Recipe

Foto von Ivan Torres auf Unsplash

Description

A classic burger is a simple yet delicious sandwich made with a beef patty, lettuce, tomato, and other toppings of your choice. It's a staple of American cuisine and can be customized to suit any taste.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Burger buns
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Cheese slices
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill or skillet to medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into patties and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Grill the patties for about 4-5 minutes on each side, or until cooked to your liking.
  4. Place a cheese slice on top of each patty during the last minute of cooking.
  5. Toast the burger buns on the grill for a minute or two.
  6. Assemble the burger by placing the patties on the bottom buns, adding lettuce, tomato, and onion.
  7. Top with the remaining buns and serve immediately.
