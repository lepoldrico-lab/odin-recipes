Pizza Recipe

Foto von Ivan Torres auf Unsplash

Description

A classic pizza is a delicious dish made with a crispy crust, tomato sauce, and various toppings. It's a popular choice for casual dining and can be customized to suit any taste.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Olives
  • Bell peppers
  • Oregano

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top of the sauce.
  5. Add pepperoni, olives, bell peppers, and any other desired toppings.
  6. Sprinkle oregano over the toppings for extra flavor.
  7. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Remove from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
